## Lomda-wide rules (גורף)

- Two tries on every question; the retry text is "זו אינה התשובה, נסו שוב." + "תוכלו ללחוץ על כפתור העזרה כדי לקבל רמז."; a help button on every question shows the help strip's text.
- Forward arrow and back arrow throughout; PLAY appears after 3 s.
- Equal weight for every question; grade 70 or more is "בוצע בהצלחה" (reported by the system).
- Animations (not videos) on slides 2, 24, 27, 29, 32.
